Applied philosophy

Some may say philosophy is too abstract. Indeed if you dig too deep, it can be incredibly abstract and confusing.

Thus, APPLIED philosophy is when you apply it into daily life. It helps connect ideas, solve problems, discover fallacies and can be an interesting cognitive science exercise.

Examples

Examples include stoicism and pragmatism, speaking from my experience.
And of course, hedonism is the backbone of most actions, though this can be psychological too.
