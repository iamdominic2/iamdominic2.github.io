Imposter syndrome

Fear of being a fraud despite being a high achiever, or when family members or friends are very different from the person. Alternatively, it can happen in high-pressure places like the education system. Leads to self-doubt and stress. It has the sense of fooling everyone, like a fraud. However, I see it as fake. I see it as the Dunning-Kruger effect and a societal bias that the successful should not "show off" to "intimidate" those still working hard to achieve.
If there is even the slightest sense of punishment or looking foolish, or where there is a fear of not living up to what the self really is, for instance, when getting asked one question about their expertise and may get it wrong, even though it's a very small sample size, isn't it natural that this term can exist?
Rather than being so much above your group, which may seem like a threat to social conformity, they act "normal", though I personally have not experienced being kicked out of a group for being "too good". Rather, basic things may not entertain you anymore, leading to you feeling weird which paradoxically can bring about more stress despite high ability, which is the exact essence of this syndrome.
