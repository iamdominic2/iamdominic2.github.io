Color psychology

People are easily manipulated by colors, I think this is likely to be a leftover from millions of years of evolution, where humans needed to identify danger (wild animals) and gain (food), but it can work against modern humans.
Eg. Just turning a word doc red text and with a bright red background will make it seem scary.
Or for traffic lights or X/Tick: Red is bad, green is good, yellow is warning
It’s from evolution, it’s from society, and it’s from pattern recognition indeed. It likely won’t change anytime in the near future.
Or it could mostly just be the fact of darwinism: How do you act like a normal person without sensory input from the eyes??
