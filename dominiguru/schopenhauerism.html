Schopenhauerism

Three principles:

1. Humans are driven by the evolutionary force to survive.

2. Suffering is the trade-off for no boredom and doing work that helps with survival.

3. The world humans perceive is incomplete: it’s a subjective viewpoint with lots of holes.

An atheistic philosophy that emphasizes that the phenomenal world is merely a representation of this underlying Will, and to overcome suffering and pain.

Schopenhauer vs Suicide

Shockingly, for a man of this kind, he rejected it.

The suicider doesn't want to not be; they want to not suffer. But it affirms that they want something factually near-impossible: make it what it is, not what factually happens to them, not meeting their “standards”.

Pragmatically, you should lower your “standards” to zero.

Suicide is an act of passion, and passion is the purest expression of the Will. Therefore, suicide is a surrender to have the Will somehow exceed the biological desire to survive, and as most doubt their action several tens or hundreds of times if they do it, if at all, it just affirms the strength of the biological will to survive, which, for a person trying to prove how futile life is, is a completely counterintuitive act. I will continue to exist as a matter of fact, but I will refuse to give the Will the satisfaction of my passion for death. I will be the Natural and Neutral Observer of my own biological shenanigans until the machine naturally runs out of fuel.

Proof of biological will to live: The clash between the societal will to live and the biological will to live. Then why won’t babies do a suicide even when they know literally zero language? They cry all the time, their needs not being properly met, and they lack many basic human functions, like walking. It must be quite a bad life currently, you know. Thus, the biological will to live takes the clash.
