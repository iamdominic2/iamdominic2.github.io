Cognitive empathy

Thinking, saying, and caring in a way that is more logical towards others, not much about emotion.
Actually trying to psychologically understand the other person instead of just feeling affectionate towards them.
Cognitive empathy is rare… because emotions wire human beings in Darwinism, and abstract ideas are relatively new in comparison. Most people actually don’t like using their logic in daily life, as it drains energy, and the lack of it will cause cognitive fatigue. Consumption of a precious resource, in socialization, is seen as an antithesis of what socializing is supposed to be, like doing fun activities together and having a satisfying time, not a demanding one, so unfortunately, I think this is rarely seen.
