Disproof of the statement “a picture is worth a thousand words”

I will disprove by counterexample; photos have their limitations.
Can a photo show the passage of time or location? Not really.
Eg. I woke up at home, and one hour later I went to the supermarket.
The element of time passing and the element of location change cannot really be photographed with a single shot. And also it does not show the inner minds of humans (in fact, a forced fake smile is common to trick viewers)
Also, sounds are not there. Videos are more useful. However, they are still popular due to the low effort needed for "normal" pictures.
