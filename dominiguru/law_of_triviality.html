Law of triviality

Trivial arguments are more common than important ones! For instance, let's take a UI designer group. Everyone has their favorite colors, or sizes, or pixels, for things like button placements.

There's also the reverse of nobody warning to deal with complex things. So often the one that is suggesting goes uncontested.
You can frequently see this even at group projects at school, and such. Perhaps the best thing to do is to just allocate proportionally to time/importance.

Fredkin's paradox

The more close alternatives are, the harder to chose between them. Same thing.
