Affective empathy

Feeling what others feel.
But biologically, I don’t actually want to feel anything that can affect my judgement when doing critical thinking. (Spinoza again: slaves to external causes)
Neither do I want to feel like the homeless.
It just causes more pain, and it doesn’t actually solve, or head towards, solving issues. (Except in the rare case where someone else notices the pain and spreads the word fast, but it’s actually rare.)
It’s a negative stimulus that I didn't create and cannot immediately fix. I wish to be stoic.
Cognitive empathy is better because I can actually think of ways to solve their problem, or accept the problem for what is, without being overwhelmed by emotions. I feel what I feel myself, not what others feel.
It’s not that strong in me.
But it depends on the definition of what even is a “feeling”. I still think my biology demands a bit of it.
Because I can feel anti-logic and tough times and authenticity.
It’s the altruism problem: duplicating pain, which is bad according to utilitarianism.
Without a resource or viable strategy to solve problems, it’s pointless redundancy.
