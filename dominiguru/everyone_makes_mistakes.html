Everyone makes mistakes

For nobody is “perfect”; otherwise, life would be boring determinism!
Clichés aside, seriously, let’s not hate accidents more than intentional acts of malice; those two can be distinguished by viewing their intent. I used to hate teachers’ mistakes, but now I just have indifference towards them as it’s just natural. Even I do so. The number of mistakes made should decrease with time due to experience, though, which is nice.
Welp, it perhaps took courage for me to accept my mistakes, and others’ mistakes too, for what they are. But I’m glad mistakes usually get remedied in the end. Common mistakes indeed include forgetting things, even for teachers.

Circular loop

Schools cause stakes (grade losses) due to mistakes while also saying that they should not feel bad for making mistakes. Just one example how they shift blame from the system to the student.
