Ideas for a happier and freer society

  • No forced school beyond primary - no sleep deprivation. Knowledge no longer becomes a trade for grades - just love.
  • Universal basic food (there really is enough!) in automated chains, perhaps just like water supply. Money would thus cause less inequality.
  • Universal housing, and if unviable, more places for the homeless to sleep (along with a request for less noise as described later)
  • If that occurs, even those who prefer to rest - or want to avoid work - can have the means to live. It will reduce the stress of serving, having the risk of failing and being fired - or learning, having the potential of being punished with a bad grade
  • Build socialization rooms all around the streets. Can close the door such that additional stranger awkwardness is gone. Each room inside the structure has just 2 chairs, and a theme of what the person wants to talk about can be changed at any time (for the first one to find a fit). No charge. Can exit anytime.
  • Loud noises ban - the world needs peace.

The human brain without school or work might be more creative and bright!

Imagine actually experiencing freedom. Think about what it would be. School denied this for nearly everyone!
