Toxic negativity

When you hear negative things over and over, it's easy to paint a bad image of the world. It can also make you feel guilty or liable, even for things you have zero control over, like politics or nature.
The negative things can change over and over, and it can also persist similarly, like the fundamental nature of death. You might want to solve negativity quickly, but the problem is that it cannot be that fast! Have skepticism when people tell you negative things are important (even if they ARE, pragmatically, they may not be worth your time)
