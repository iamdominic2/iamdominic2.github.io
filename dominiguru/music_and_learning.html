Music and learning

Some sources say music hinders learning.
But I would say that it only hinders deep work. Trust your Darwin brain!

What's more important is Pleasure.

You can stop music at any time.
However, restricting music (all) in the first place is damaging. Freedom is important too!

If you only study with music, or are willing to study longer with music, then go for it! While also being aware of the status quo bias: once in a while, evaluate your workflow.
