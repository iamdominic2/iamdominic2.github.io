Clash of Noise

This comes from one day a school student realized there simply is not enough way, not enough power to change a problem that plagued him since childhood. Perhaps he did not have teachers to blame and wanted to "justify" what is unjustifiable: uncontrolable noises leading ti autistic noise suffering and non-pleasurableness!

I have investigated this for so long, yet in this eternal doom, I have found no solution.
It will be a prickly prodigious problem until I leave school.
It’s my desire to be quiet versus
the others’ desire to speak even over the teacher to foster “human connection”, and to avoid loneliness, pain and suppression.
However, if I hear something, it naturally diverts my attention away from the teacher to the source of the noise.
I can only endure it to a limited extent before it starts psychologically hurting my brain.
Even with my mental fortitude, dealing with it for six hours per day is surprisingly difficult.
The biggest problem here is that there’s no solution that doesn’t create more problems:
Get rid of the cause of the noise: Of course I can’t murder my classmates, or cut their larynxes off, I would end up in jail
Drop all my subjects and self-study: I might not be able to and I might not know how to learn those subjects perfectly
Wear earphones: It doesn’t reduce unwanted noise fully, and it also suppresses the teachers’ noise, makes me look lazy, and some teachers hate it. (if an exception was made for me, others would naturally also complain which would likely lead to more chaos and trouble. Plus high end ones are too expensive. I don’t want to pay to solve problems others cause.
Destroy my ears: I don’t want to lose my hearing totally, it would make life more troublesome.
Change seat: The classroom is a closed space with echo, impact negligible, plus most teachers reject self-made seat changes.
Unlike other things, silence cannot really be created… unless everyone co-operates and there is bound to be at least one breaker of it. (Some other things that only work if everyone does it include lining up, side of lane to drive, and which side is going up/going down in stairs)

Why this type of noise is the most unpleasant: It ruins my focus as it’s hard to suppress only the students’ noises without suppressing the teachers’, it causes teachers to scold repetitively, it’s variable (sometimes quiet, sometimes noisy) which actually messes with the my brain’s hedonic adaptation capabilities, and it’s chaotic as the brain cannot comprehend anything useful from the noise which ruins the purpose of communication. It’s not that I can’t hear, it’s that it’s painful.
Thus, I just painfully and stoically endure this barrage of hell’s wrath from foul mouths. Every day. I am surviving not because of my classmates, but despite them.

Thus I mainly learn by myself after school. Which kills the point of school in the first place. Yay.
