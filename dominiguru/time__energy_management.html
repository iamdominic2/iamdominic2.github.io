Time management

I don't like this phrase. It implies you can control time, which you cannot - it just flows away and leads to the unhealthy belief that it is a good idea to sleep less. When perhaps saving time was never the goal of life in the first place if you had enough time! (doing negative satisfaction things more isn't a good thing)

What time management implies

We can be perfectly logical and optimal people who don't need breaks, where things don't go wrong, and energy levels don't vary. Time management is quite silly: it assumes some things are not good. E.g. YouTube. Despite the fact if u look carefully there are times where there are no tasks necessary and you value YouTube right now!

"Waste of time" - but isn't it the goal to satisfy life by doing what u want (implies there's a better activity o do)

Energy management

Reducing consumption of energy, for instance in boring activities like school, to save for other activities like freedom.

Time management now cliché?

Time management sounds a bit cliché now; is it because it’s just wrong?
As I hear an excuse for not working or studying, “I’m tired.” or “I’m fed up.” JUST NAP. NAP. It’s either 10-15 minutes, or 90 minutes, anywhere in between likely leads to tiredness. Seriously, I would recommend a nap if you are at home. You have time when you wake up. Considering how much time people use for non-academics, I would say people pretty much have all the time in the world… yet complain about a lack of time. It might be an addiction, but it might also be doing high-energy-demand/high-dopamine activities like sports and videogames before homework makes the person exhausted and unmotivated due to increasing the motivation baseline, so low energy to do work, meaning low output from the person due to low cognitive load. Lack of time is often used as a social excuse to not do what is supposed to be done (in reality, they lack energy management)
Society views napping during the day as laziness because they prioritize visibility over output. In reality, if I am alone in my room revising, what I appear to be doing doesn’t matter one bit!
(One drawback of napping is that it can affect the time I go to normal sleep, but I think it's better than groggily doing a past paper. It’s possible to return it back to normal by adjusting sleep time earlier and earlier over a few days.)
