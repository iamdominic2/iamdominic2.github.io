The infamous habit

It exists: YouTube, Instagram, TikTok... It's infamous for a reason! Social media! The biggest time sink! Yet, people (even me on YouTube) tend to do that. Reasons include the fact that there is little energy consumption and perceived value. Even if you know it, you still do it. Gratification (short-term) comes. School is the antithesis of doomscrolling: Long hours of sitting or working.

Rise of short-form content

It consumes the attention span of most children and teenagers, as there is no end and it is incredibly easy to use your fingers to scroll, and the algorithm draws you in. The infinite scroll removes the natural stops that exist in books or movies. It’s close to the lowest effort, highest pleasure things that humans can do - even if it actually doesn’t do anything useful.

Fatigue and impulses

The fact that students come back home tired and wanting a break can also contribute to this. Once a fatigued mind starts, it's hard to switch.

Controlled doomscrolling!

Theoretically, it can be a neutral action! There technically is no loss other than time, and it can be a source of great pleasure. In fact, it can be that the school has a dogma that it is important, and thus other things are a waste of time. However, if you do have excess time, and not really having other more important tasks, there really isn't much loss... It depends on the perspective!
