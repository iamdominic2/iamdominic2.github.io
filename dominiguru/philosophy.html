Philosophy

The systematic, rational, and critical study of fundamental questions regarding existence, knowledge, ethics, reason, mind, and language. This is fun (FOR ME, maybe not for you), rare, and useful, unlike almost everything else in school, possibly due to the overjustification effect. It has to be framed very carefully, with precise language and also as a factual way of thinking, neither positively nor negatively biased, of course, to the limits of the human who is writing the philosophical ideas or concepts themselves.
