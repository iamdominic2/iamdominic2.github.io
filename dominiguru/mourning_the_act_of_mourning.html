Mourning the act of mourning

Humans mourn deaths that aren’t personally related to them. I think that just not mind it and trying to somewhat personally ignore it is going to lead to more stable emotions through stoicism, yet many do care. I guess it’s perhaps because humans have evolved to be (perhaps overly) social creatures. Evolutionary issue. As if anything can be done by a weakling like me…
Wait, in fact, if you mourn every single death in the whole world, the death count will rise up faster than your mind can even search up the cause, so thus as a conclusion I don’t think mourning deaths is that productive at all. Still, humans ARE conditioned to think death is bad, and they are social creatures. That's the whole jest of it!
