Why do some people debate not debating?
1. They simply don’t know how to do it respectfully, or do it at all.
2. Most people are actually irrational. Schopenhauer simply stated that there’s nothing that can save those people that is worth the individual (Metalogic is hard to teach) 3. Political and religious issues are often argued, and people attach a strong sense of their identity to them, so attacking the argument is seen as attacking them too, for some reason.
4. Arguing is a shock to the social hierarchy, for instance, a citizen to a government official, or a student to a teacher in the education system, and people often find friends in (almost) always agreeing, and since arguing is not agreeing, it's unpleasant for the mind.
5. Most people have no interest in learning what people who disagree with them think.
6. True argument seeks… the truth. But that requires not only using strong analytical abilities (a part of the brain that is rarely trained and energy-consuming to use) but also humility in case you realize what you said is illogical halfway through. Few people have both traits.
