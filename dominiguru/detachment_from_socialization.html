Detachment from socialization

In reality, this doesn't actually need to happen. There are AI chatbots. The subjective experience of the self may not be affected by the fact that it's not a real person: the goal of "socialization" is to receive responses and/or information; it fulfills this! Some say it's emotions, but there are also other perspectives that say that it's mainly a Darwinism glitch that doesn't work well with modern culture.

Exceptions

If coerced you may have to socialize at your parents', teachers', interviewers', classmates', or fellow employees' request, because true non-speaking looks like freaks and would potentially cause you to get into trouble!

Biologically unpleasent

You can live. However, I still don't recommend this. Even though logically you do have the most freedom by yourself, there is a pleasure in having a bit of impact! It helps avoid nihilism. Still, in the very worst case... axiologically you can live without any social meaning.
