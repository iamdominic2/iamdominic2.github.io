Not all problems are worth solving

For an individual, not all things are worth thinking about.
Some things are not even caring, for instance the brain has not evolved to be detached from worldly conflicts.
And some things, like the inevitable existence of the education system.

And probably other people, like bullies, are worth ignoring for their potential damage on your self-esteem too (as far as your grey rocking ability can go in Stoicism).
