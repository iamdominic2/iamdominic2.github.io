AI

Literally works by probability and semantic distances. Especially if what you write is not a question.

Social media

Plot twist: humans too

Most friends are like this by nature. Because otherwise it would be stranger and stranger; that's where the word "stranger' comes from. Strange to you because it does not mirror enough of your beliefs.

Why?

Obviously, on a basic level, if you found something/someone totally against your culture, or even a different language, you would be skeptical. However, the best way to expand tone concept network is by similar not same stuff. On the other hand, a bubble just repeats what you know.
