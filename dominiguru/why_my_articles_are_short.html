Why my articles are short

1. I am the sole writer, so I don't have other people's viewpoints. However, I do try to talk about what I think other people think, which can be different from what other people actually think.
2. I also don't do sources. I find them a rather boring thing to do because I don't wanna make this too academic to the point you fall asleep! As said before, if you really want to know, search online or email me!
3. Wikipedia articles are too long! Most give up after a while (in reality, it's the sunk cost fallacy. The things that are at the top: the lead section, are usually almost all you need to know. They read the rest just to forget it quickly.)
As such, I will spare you all the excessive words, and thus I will also be able to write more because less effort per article. A win-win!
