Gaslightning

For instance, claiming that something has not happened even though it did, or the opposite, until the other person gives up, due to tedium or emotions, and then is forced to believe a false version of reality. Corrupting the narrative and memories inside the victim.
It’s manipulative, it’s crazy, and it drives logic and memory totally out of the window and into the dark realms. Yet, some people do this because they don’t want to admit some wrong or just want to torture other people. It can actually cause emotional and mental problems, and it can actually be hard to recognize in those times of pressure. So I simply don’t want this to happen.
Documentation is in fact one of the greatest protections to gaslighting, and so is indifference, for instance, saying “Well, I recognize what it is from your perspective, but I’m sticking to my version, thanks.” or heck, saying nothing!
