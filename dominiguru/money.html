How to save money

Cancel unnecessary subscription services, avoid luxury goods, and find the cheapest options!
But beware of your own psychology too: marketing tricks like limited-time coupons may lead to impulsive decisions! They just want a sale, even if not at full price, because a small profit is better than no profit.
If you use a Value/Price spreadsheet (even though Value will be subjective), you will have a better idea that Cheaper is better. You may be surprised at what you can drop!!

Psychology of money

Some really like to save it, and others really like to spend it. Some even lavishly show off their wealth (who cares about social validation)! Well, perhaps the ones who like to save are the ones that didn’t earn that much in the first place…

Discount psychology

The more urgent, or the higher the discount (Regardless of whether the discounted price in isolation is a good one or not!) the more the demand skyrockets because it makes the customer feel like they got a great deal. Humans usually only compare the original price and think they are "saving", not "spending"…

Survival and freedom

The more survival you have the more you sacrifice your freedom.
The basic act of having the chance requires school, and job, but you do want food to survive, and that needs money.
You can be unemployed and have basically as much freedom as you have in this society. (other than power positions)
On the other hand, prisoners pretty much will survive due to "free food", but have no freedom whatsoever.
Additionally, hunter-gatherers of wild animals may have lots of freedom, but suffer acute stress, yet have risks to their survival because the amount of animals they kill on a given day is variable (and some animals can retaliate and destroy their life instantly).
