External influences

External influences are factors that come from outside the person, like an environmental disaster as an extreme example. However, the most common case is likely other people (and recently, technology). Other people can do coercion and impact the person's critical thinking abilities and their sense of esteem.

External influences can also negatively impact freedom, for instance, if society implicitly suggests that there is a good life, is there free will to do what one internally wants?
