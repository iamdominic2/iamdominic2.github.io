Pluralism

Also known as Eclecticism. Having multiple philosophies or religions.
If others have already made their own philosophy, what’s the point? The point is that one philosophy certainly won't fit all. The closest one is pragmatism, but humans may also have social needs and cannot simply "do what works" all the time.
As such, no single philosophy fits all people.
So one needs to weigh, mix, and match daily activities, depending on the presence of others and many other factors, etc. Experience helps here, and also do a cost-benefit analysis to see what a philosophy can contribute and is worth following or not.

Drawbacks

The weightings you give to each philosophy can be arbitrary, and it can add to decision fatigue, as you add more and more philosophies...
