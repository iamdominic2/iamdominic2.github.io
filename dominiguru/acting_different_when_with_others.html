Acting different when with others

This is because the person will be observed and judged based on what society collectively agrees is good and bad. For instance, let’s take Masturbation - pleasurable on your own, but a red flag when with others!

People self-monitor and self-censor and also get influenced to impress people, build an aura of charisma (perhaps by holding luxury goods), and thus they are no longer their authentic true self.
