Neutrality

Basically, trying to be objective and not be skewed or fall to human bias.

Neutrality's societal views

Not taking sides in a conflict, dispute, or war, often involving impartiality and non-alignment. Usually, neutrality is ethnically and morally neutral by definition, yet sometimes it is seen as cold or indecisive. And on social media, where the extremes are supreme, it is rarely considered as it doesn’t ragebait or make people excited.
