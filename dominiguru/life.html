Life

The opposite of death. You need to be able to have a response to a stimulus, or the firing of synapses. It is what you are having right now, provided you are human and not AI data scraper! That is its whole objective meaning. Embrace its absurdity or something.

Burden of living

Technically, all humans are a burden. Burden in this context means resource consumption. Food and oxygen, for instance. But that doesn’t mean that humans will overcome the biological will to live anytime soon. And it’s not like they are at risk of extinction, as food is mass-produced in factories and oxygen is renewed by plants. Still, isn't it better to be alive!
