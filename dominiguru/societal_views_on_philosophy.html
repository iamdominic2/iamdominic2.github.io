Societal views on philosophy

It is sometimes seen as a rebellion; in fact, there are historical cases where governments tried to ban any beliefs that go against them. Or sometimes, it can be too brutally honest, especially Schopenhauerist beliefs that say that the world is basically in eternal pain and bound by a will to live even though there are more problems than happiness in the human realm. Most people rely on a delusion of constant progress and happiness to have motivation to continue to live in a peaceful life.
Socrates was executed for "corrupting the youth." In reality, he was teaching them to dismantle the trash arguments of the ruling class.
When a person adopts a consistent logical system, they become immune to standard manipulation that, for example, blames a person for their failures due to “lack of effort” when sometimes it’s more so caused by external factors.
Society demands total transparency and shared beliefs; philosophy allows for a hidden, internal kingdom that the law cannot touch.
Critics often claim that deep philosophy leads to anhedonia or a lack of contribution to the zero-sum game of the economy. They fear that if everyone realized the absurdity of the system, the unpaid labor and the factory-style schools would collapse. Philosophy is controversial because it reveals that the rock Sisyphus pushes is actually optional if one is willing to face the consequences of stopping.
Some may believe critical thinkers (who do not follow the mainstream) are dangerous people; in reality, they feel scared because it exposes the flaws of traditional thinking, and most people simply learn from their surroundings without processing how valid the average is, due to aiming to make human behavior predictable.
