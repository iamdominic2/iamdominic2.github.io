The unambitious

Is fame good? Is innovation good?
Social concepts? Both can be beneficial, but can also be drawbacks - eg., gigantic work hours = gigantic money, not necessarily beneficial.
Is not working a moral failing? Fair? Is it actually bad to not chance hustle?
Is failing just not living up to others’ “expectations”? If you need to fulfill others’ expectations, doesn’t it cut into freedom? (Unambitious: common insult to them is that not working to full potential)
But isn’t full potential painful? Perhaps losses are greater than gains, in terms of money, energy, time, brain… Welp ,jobs nowadays do
n't want people just doing their job; they need more (!?)
Stripping societal ideas, why is it even moral to force self-improvement?
Achieving is good, but is not achieving bad? Well, perhaps the person wants to do other stuff… or is burnt out… or sees no gains! So don’t always force people…! Or it can be… that material success (money,y etc) is a waste of time. Judging by achievements is a big mistake because of external validati...
Worth cannot simply be measured by money, or political power, or social power… It can just be luck, and some don’t even care much…
Not everyone can be a winner, because it’s relative, yet society constantly says not to compare to others - and decides that phrase doesn’t mean anything in practicality because again, competition with other humans continues to exist as long as the human race exists, which is just paradoxical
