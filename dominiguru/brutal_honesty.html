Honesty

Not lying. Society is not fully honest, because there are, in fact, softening jokes… Stuff should totally not be taken blatantly, as most human communication is not factual; it's based on opinions.

Brutal honesty

Even though some would like it, most won’t… I think silence is a better alternative because it avoids you having to decide whether it’s a good idea to lie to them.

Brutal honesty: obvious example of consequences

Employer: "What is your motivation to study?"

Me:

"I need an A* in Business so that you won't consider me not academically capable enough."

Employer:

"Anything else?"

Me:

"The education system is so boring, so I didn't really study my subjects for pleasure; I just want to use my grades to increase the chance of getting a job, thus getting money. No money means biological death due to lack of ability to buy food."

Employer:

"Why even us?"

Me:

"No particular reason. I just applied for 600 jobs to increase the chance of getting at least one. I have an A* in Business, and one condition for your company is a sufficiently good educational background."

Employer:

"... Well then, have a nice day, sire."
