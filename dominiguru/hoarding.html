Hoarding

Wanting to collect as much as possible, without "using" it up (if applicable.)

Mentality

When there is a part of something but not all of it, an irrational will to get the remaining parts kicks in as there is a fear of missing out as things disappear over time (even if they don’t, life is finite), causing humans to attempt to do so.
Pragmatically, most collectors’ items are of little pragmatic value (if ignoring resale) so it can be the sunk cost fallacy taking effect.

E.g.. Pokémon card collectors. Due to the irrational will of needing a copy of everything. Or maybe it’s resell value, or fear of missing out even when the princes are close to extortionate.

Possible cause: Fear of things being lost to time

For instance, shops offering limited time products leads more sales.
Admittedly, I do have this, but only to a limited capacity. Obviously I will also look at the price and whether it’s suitable, if it is cheap or free, I will consider.
Technically, life is also limited-time, but is that too deep? Leading to people irrationally cherishing the time they are alive? For me, being alive until death is just a fact by tautology, nothing more to it.
