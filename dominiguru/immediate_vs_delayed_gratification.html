Immediate gratification

Feeling the fruits of your actions right away! Like in the tribal evolution days, you could sense the meat of an animal you killed immediately!

Immediate vs delayed gratification

Most brains value immediate gratification a lot more, like scrolling on short-form content all day long. But the delayed gratification is usually a lot bigger, and more significant pragmatically. However, if the gratification is of the same intensity, it’s not a bad idea to just do it now to free up one thought from the mind. There is a recency bias (Downplaying the past), however sometimes it's not even a bias but a reality, or reality can be chaotic.
