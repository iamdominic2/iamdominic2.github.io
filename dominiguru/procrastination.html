Procrastination

Procrastination occurs when the will to start a task isn’t strong enough, or the brain is tired of exerting effort on a specific task.
You usually delay a task you hate doing. Natural but hated, especially for stuff related to the irrational education system!

I personally think it is a psychological energy and capacity problem. Pretty much no human works for extended hours, and they all take some time off, so they are all pretty much distracted in some way. Also, interleaving helps.
But, indeed, having mental health problems does affect work efficiency, so I’m not saying that being a total productivity freak is a good thing.
It comes from the brain seeing that there is little value to do something it hates. When something is hell, not doing it is more pleasurable in comparison. Only when the sense of urgency is high, for instance homework deadlines (possibly due to restrictions on freedom otherwise), will the brain actually start doing it.

Productive procrastination

I heard it's doing something small just to avoid the big thing. The danger is that longer-duration tasks can be postponed indefinitely! However, usually, as long as you get things done before deadlines, it should be fine if there is little stress.
Procrastination isn’t always a bad thing, sometimes it’s logical, for instance just having an ultra-bad mood so even working would consume more energy for less output. In that case, just take a break (assuming it’s not urgent homework with a deadline)!
For instance, trying so hard to perfect a study routine, or study aesthestics for social media instead of you know, "actually" studying. What a classic.

Problem with it

At the same time people really like micro-tweaks and doing things that don't take that much time but bring about benefits. Some count it as productive procrastination; others don't. It's tricky.

Procrasination paradox

It is when everything not related to external demands is labelled as procrastination, and everything indirectly linked to it being labelled as productive procrastination. The person thus may self-blame leading to even more problems.

Solutions

Calm yourself down, or take a nap. Best (yet a bit tough) option is to bore yourself in a stimulus-less room for so long you get the urge to do the thing you hate (like revision)
