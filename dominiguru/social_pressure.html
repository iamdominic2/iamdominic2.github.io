Social pressure

The influence exerted by peers, family, or society that compels individuals to align their thoughts, behaviors, and actions with group norms, even when pragmatically those thoughts, behaviors, and actions aren’t totally rational or beneficial. (Sometimes they can be fallacies)
And the pain of exclusion is too much to handle for many people, not just socially extroverted people, but also people who are reliant on family for money support, etc.
It clearly affects thinking. In some cases, it can be coercion too! Thus sometimes this is not that healthy.

How to avoid social pressure

Recognize the self as an independent entity and adopt a pragmatic approach to problem-solving and decision-making, and recognize that society can SOMETIMES be totally wrong, as great innovators and thinkers get famous for the reason that they defy traditional ideology.
To avoid the fallacy, treat others' suggestions and noise as such, and sometimes dare to admit that they are, in fact, likely wrong. Empathy and guilt are usually just trickery, although they exist for a reason; in fact, they can be abused too.
