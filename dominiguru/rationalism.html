Rationalism

Philosophical view that reason and logic are the primary source and test of knowledge. I follow this view, and it’s the source of both knowledge and problems. Deduct solutions from logic, similar to mathematical proofs, where there are lessons to be learned that are not just from sensory experience. It’s philosophy before philosophy existed, in a sense. Most of the arguments for and against logic apply to this, too, so see that page for more about it.
