Misanthropy

A view that the world is inherently predatory, and can lead to personality disorders. It can be disappointment in others, or the rules that the self is bound upon by. In fact, there is rarely a solution to this, and may become true on its own due to social karma or social Darwinism, or through internalization of false beliefs resulting in hating everyone else wanting to say otherwise; once a person becomes , others will then also become towards them, making a tautological loop.
