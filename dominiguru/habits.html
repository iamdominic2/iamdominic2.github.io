Habits

Things done often, almost every day. Building them is pretty important, and pretty good. Assuming, of course, it is a positive thing in the first place (bad habits can be learned too, more often unconsciously)

Power of habits

Lots of work done long-term. For instance, if you code every day, you may become good at it quickly.

Micro-habits

Small rules to prevent fatigue. Over time you can increase them. A form of incrementalism.
