Diminishing returns

Where the returns of doing an action decrease as more is put in.
In mathematical terms, it's like a log-scale.
This is likely one reason why we take breaks or stop after a while. Attention has diminishing returns.
It applies to almost everything: you start finding easy/visible errors and have to hunt for the tiny areas you can improve.

Examples

For instance, homeless people are happier to get a $1000 money bill than Elon Musk, who wouldn't care one breath.
