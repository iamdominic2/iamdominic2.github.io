Theology

The study of whether God exists or not. Within philosophy this is a pretty big topic in general, yet one that is almost impossible to comprehend deeply, as you cannot observe God scientifically!

Theological truism: probably not logically valid

God created the world, including the Bible, and Bible is always true and says God created the world!

God v.s. Science

This is where the difficulty lies.
The problem is that God is non-observable yet is said to exist.
God being not scientifically observational means you cannot prove existence
But he could exist beyond visible universe scope, or be "unobservable" yet existent like the concept of gravity, which means you cannot prove non-existence!
Thus I don’t think it will end, because there aren’t any proofs for or against God’s existence that are not heavily flawed in some ways. So, keep wondering about it!
