Control of World

External forces affect my freedom, my "success"...
World getting harder, less can be done, but if less can be controlled, control what can be controlled (focus pretty much effort on that)
It’s stupid to blame others… nothing will change, but optimizing self is more important? Income rising much slower than inflation… easier to find jobs in the past due to less applicant competition… The value of degrees goes down.
In fact, it might be because the easier it is, the less work people will need to do.
There is to-an-extent large control of self, and a bit of influence to others.
The world as a whole though, is shaken by chaotic reality, Thus I conclude the control of the objective world is very minimal but the subjective world is slightly more, though there can still be unpredictable stuff sometimes.
