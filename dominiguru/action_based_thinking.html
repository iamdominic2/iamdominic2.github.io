Action based thinking

Thinking, instead of how to OVERCOME wants and emotions (which usually leads to a stagnation), just: what actions to take to move towards the desires and what doesn’t? Overthinking, sometimes, is a bit of a wrong direction.
This is because, wants like starting a new company, making money, are also WANTS, just like playing videogames!! So if you head towards your wants of videogames, you can apply it to doing actions too! However, basically, the one thing I would suggest: Be aware of the bias that you cannot act optimally all the time.
