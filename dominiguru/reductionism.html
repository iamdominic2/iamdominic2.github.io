Reductionism

Reducing ideas into simpler or more fundamental phenomena. For instance, reducing questionable human decisions to simply brain behavior/psychology and not overthinking. Honestly, hedonism might be one of these, reducing life into pain and pleasure. Most things can be reduced to very simple concepts. This is one of the great things about learning, even if "scientifically illiterate" (however, still, some say in learning, it's the pleasure of knowing that counts)
