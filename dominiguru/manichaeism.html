Manichaeism

Theological argument for that there are two Gods, one infinitely good, one infinitely evil! If there is a Good God, to explain the presence of evil, a Dark God is likely to also exist.
Dark God’s Power = -Infinity
Good God’s Power = Infinity
But because (-Infinity)+(Infinity) is undefined, not zero, that’s why good and evil fluctuate all the time!
God making a clone of itself: Infinity - Infinity = undefined chaos, even though any other number would make it zero.
It’s somewhat like the Chaos Theory
At the very least, it makes more sense than the Christian God.
