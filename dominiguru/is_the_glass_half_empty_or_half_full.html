Is the glass half empty or half full?

A false logical dilemma about whether a glass is half full or half empty (when it's both). It's said that half empty is seen as pessimism, while half full is seen as optimism. However, I would argue for neither, as it is more so realism. Optimism and pessimism are comparing thoughts versus reality: half full and half empty both adhere to truth! So I think this is a false saying.
