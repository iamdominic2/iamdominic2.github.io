7. Fight-and-3D-self! (A pun on "Free the self") (Sep 2022)



A custom 3D raycasting maze-runner with real-time monster combat, potion management, but primarily involves a rush to the exit, especially in the later stages! Navigate tight corridors, defeat enemies with a variety of weapons, and most of all, remember to keep your health topped up throughout the mazes!

10. The crime of no return (Jan 2023)



An experimental JRPG rendered inside a single text list interface. Battle your worst nemeses, manage status effects, laugh at the dialogue that somehow repesents modern society, and explore what technically is a full RPG loop built under extreme design constraints - using only raw text strings and emojis for graphics!
