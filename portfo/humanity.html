A world against humanity

You are in a world where there is lots of crime.
You hear everyone believe that an all-loving powerful being has created it, yet it is filled with evil.
Not only theft and public indecency,
But also murder and arson.
The people who ruin other’s lives are the worst.
You wonder, how can greatness come?
He replies, never.
You are in a world where everyone thinks differently from you.
All the good is fake, and people try to make you believe it’s real;
All the evil is real, yet people try to make you suppress it like it’s fake.
We are in a place with no future,
And the things to be proud of keep getting smaller.
You wonder, how can you change their beliefs?
He replies, not at all.
You are in a world where our future is uncertain.
You know how mankind has ruined the world for the worse,
And you also know how acts of “God” have caused destruction.
We speak so much about climate change and such,
Yet, you feel an impending sense of doom because nobody is willing to take action.
You wonder, how can it be fixed?
He replies, no way.

You are in a world where there is prejudice.
You think about the times when people were biased towards you.
You get headaches realizing that now, the world hates men more,
But people just try to deny all of it.
Yet, unconsciously, everyone is like that.
You wonder, how can it be equalized?
He replies, not possible.

You are in a world where there is poverty.
You see all the homeless people down the street.
But you don’t give them any money.
And you believe you have to sort yourself out first.
The debts from education and rent are just too much.
You wonder, how can it be negated?
He replies, never can.

You are in a world where there is war.
Every day, thousands of people die due to Russia’s horrific actions.
Not only do you fear chemical and biological weapons,
But also nuclear weapons and weapons of mass destruction.
Every day, you have a nightmare about the world.
You wonder, how can the world be at peace?
He replies, never calm.

You are in a world where there is job unemployment.
You struggle to comprehend what jobs you can do.
You become anxious about whether enough money can be made.
And without money, the quality of life is like a worn-out trash can full of mud in a luxury hotel.
You collapse onto the floor, realizing the extremely high hurdles in front of you.
You wonder, how can I get a job?
He replies, no idea.

You are in a world where there is revision hell.
You see all the stacks of past papers around you on your dirty desk,
And realize that you will never get a good night’s sleep for a year.
You wonder whether it is a child labour camp or a human rights violation.
You panic, realizing a single badly done piece of paper can make you worthless, just because you haven’t tortured yourself to the fullest.
You wonder, how can such a life be worth living?
He replies, NO.
